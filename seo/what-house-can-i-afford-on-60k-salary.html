What House Can I Afford on $60,000 Salary?

Short answer: Most buyers earning $60,000 can typically afford a home between $180,000 and $240,000, depending on debt, savings, and location.

Monthly Income Breakdown

Your gross monthly income is about $5,000. Lenders usually limit housing to 28% of this.

Down Payment Reality

A 20% down payment on a $240,000 home would require roughly $48,000. Saving aggressively is essential.

Lender Rules Explained

The 28% rule limits housing costs. The 36% rule limits total debt. Student loans and car payments reduce buying power.

Realistic Price Ranges

While lenders may approve higher amounts, financial planners recommend staying near the lower end to avoid becoming house-poor.

Location Matters

In high-cost states like California or New York, $60,000 stretches far less than in Texas or Ohio.

Monthly Ownership Costs

Mortgage is only part of the story. Property taxes, insurance, maintenance, and utilities often add $400–$800+ monthly.

How to Afford More on $60,000

  • Increase income
  • Save larger down payment
  • Reduce debt
  • Buy in lower-cost markets
  • Improve credit score

Is Buying Better Than Renting?

Buying builds equity but comes with higher upfront costs. Renting may feel cheaper short-term but offers no ownership.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is $60,000 enough to buy a home? Yes in many areas, but budget carefully.

Do I need 20% down? No, but PMI increases monthly costs.

Should I wait? Depends on rates and job stability.
